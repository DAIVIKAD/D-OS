GETTING STARTED

What is D-OS?

D-OS (D-OS Money Operating System) is your personal finance command center. It helps you track expenses, income, real monthly budgets, isolated custom budgets, savings goals, recurring payments, and lending — all from a single terminal-inspired dashboard. Designed & Developed by Daivik A.D.

Dashboard overview

The Dashboard displays your core financial snapshot: today's expenses, monthly spending, savings, remaining budget, active goals, and money to receive or pay. It also features a real-time Income vs Expense chart and financial alerts.

Adding your first transaction

Tap the central + Add button in the bottom navigation (mobile) or the + Record button in the top navigation (desktop). Choose Expense or Income, select a category, enter an amount and date, then submit. Quick Amount shortcuts (₹100, ₹200, ₹500, ₹1000, ₹2000, ₹5000) are available for fast entry.

MONEY

How do I add an expense?

Navigate to Transactions (`/money`) or tap the + Add button. Select - Expense, choose an expense category (Food, Fuel, Bills, Shopping, Rent, Travel, Health, Other), enter the amount, select the transaction date, and tap Record Expense.

Recording income

Go to Transactions or tap + Add. Select + Income, choose an income category (Salary, Business, Freelance, Investment, Other), enter the amount and date, then tap Record Income.

Editing or deleting a transaction

Go to the Transactions Ledger (`/money`). In the transaction history table, each entry provides an edit (✎) and delete (⌫) action. You can update the amount, date, payment method, category, or note at any time.

BUDGETING

What is a budget?

A budget is the total spending ceiling you establish for a calendar month. D-OS compares your real-time expenses against this target, calculates your remaining allowance, and computes your recommended daily spending limit.

What are custom budgets?

Custom Budgets are dedicated financial planners for separate events or projects — like a Goa Trip (₹15,000), Wedding Shopping (₹35,000), or a festival celebration. They let you log project expenses into an isolated ledger.

Do custom budgets affect my monthly budget?

No. Custom Budgets operate in a strictly isolated event universe. Expenses logged inside a Custom Budget never increase your monthly spending metric, never decrease your monthly remaining budget, and never alter your primary Ledger cash flow.

GOALS

What are goals?

Goals help you track money you are intentionally saving toward a specific target — such as a Himalayan bike purchase, a vacation fund, or emergency savings. D-OS automatically calculates the daily, weekly, and monthly saving pace required to achieve your target date.

How do I add money to a goal?

Navigate to Goals (`/goals`). On any active goal card, enter the amount saved in the "Add Funds" form and submit. The progress percentage and remaining balance update immediately.

ANALYTICS

What is Analytics?

Analytics (`/visualize`) delivers visual reporting of your financial data: Monthly Income vs Expense comparison, Expense Allocation by category, Savings Trajectory, Monthly Budget Progress, Cumulative Cash Flow, and Goal Completion progress.

Why do some charts show "No Data Available"?

Charts require at least one recorded transaction to generate meaningful visualizations. Once you record transactions in the Ledger, each visualization populates automatically with real-time data.

RECURRING PAYMENTS

What are recurring payments?

Recurring Payments let you monitor subscriptions, rent, utility bills, and insurance premiums. Access them via More → Recurring Payments (`/recurring`) to schedule reminders for upcoming obligations.

Does D-OS automatically create recurring transactions?

No. D-OS tracks recurring payments strictly as scheduled obligations and reminders so you know what is due. You record the actual payment manually in the Ledger when the payment is made, ensuring full control over your financial records.

LEND & BORROW

How do I use Lend & Borrow?

Navigate to More → Lend & Borrow (`/lend-borrow`). Select "Money Given (I Lent)" if someone owes you money, or "Money Taken (I Borrowed)" if you owe someone. Enter the person's name, amount, and due date. Mark the record as "Paid" once settled.

SYSTEM

How do I access Settings?

Settings is accessible directly from the ⚙ gear icon in the header bar. You can update your display name, currency symbol, password, and manage custom categories with monthly limits.

How does the loading indicator work?

D-OS shows real-time loading feedback using indeterminate CRT scanning [████░░░░░░░░] during genuine server operations (e.g. `[SAVING EXPENSE...]`, `[DELETING...]`). If an operation takes longer than 2.5 seconds, it notifies you that the request is taking longer than expected. D-OS never uses fake percentages.

Why does D-OS show offline?

D-OS monitors your network connectivity in real time. If your internet connection drops, D-OS displays "OFFLINE // NO NETWORK CONNECTION DETECTED" until connectivity is restored.

How do I logout?

You can end your session anytime via the ⏻ logout button in the top navigation bar, inside More → Logout, or inside Settings → Session → Logout.
