## Cyber City Login Background

Use case: stylized-concept  
Asset type: web application login/register hero background, desktop landscape crop  
Primary request: A cinematic cyberpunk rainy city background for a personal finance app named D OS.  
Scene/backdrop: neon city skyline at night, reflective wet street, subtle rupee-symbol inspired light motifs integrated into distant signage without readable brand text.  
Subject: atmospheric cyber city environment, no people, no vehicles in foreground.  
Style/medium: high-quality digital matte painting, polished production web background.  
Composition/framing: wide 16:9 landscape, strong visual interest on left and center, darker quiet space on right for glass login forms.  
Lighting/mood: purple, blue, and pink neon glow, rain streaks, soft volumetric mist, premium but not cluttered.  
Color palette: cyberpunk purple, electric blue, magenta, deep black.  
Text: no readable text.  
Constraints: no watermark, no logo, no UI mockup, no legible words, usable as a web background, crisp details, balanced contrast.  
Avoid: faces, people, cars dominating foreground, random letters, distorted text, cluttered composition.

Saved as:

- `static/assets/backgrounds/desktop/cyber-city-login.png`
- `static/assets/backgrounds/mobile/cyber-city-login-mobile.png`
